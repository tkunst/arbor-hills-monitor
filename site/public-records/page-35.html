2005-12-09 · Arbor Hills Remediation Area · evidence · routine

CSI lab data

MDEQ laboratory analysis of seven water and compost samples from Arbor Hills Landfill (ONYX) collected in October 2005, testing for volatile organic compounds, base/neutral/acid compounds, pesticides, PCBs, and inorganic parameters. Most results are non-detected; detected compounds include trace chlorinated hydrocarbons, metals, and nutrients in ppb/ppm ranges typical of landfill leachate.

Key data point: Detected analytes include 1,1-dichloroethane (9.6 ppb, ONYX-INF), benzene (1.1 ppb, ONYX-INT), cis-1,2-dichloroethylene (16–17 ppb), and vinyl chloride (3.6–4.0 ppb); arsenic ranging 4.7–14 µg/L; lead detected at 1.8 µg/L; TOC at 18–140 mg/L across samples.
