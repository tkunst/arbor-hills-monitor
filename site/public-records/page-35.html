2006-06-16 · Arbor Hills Remediation Area · procedural · routine

Application Compliance Comments

Michigan Department of Environmental Quality Water Bureau staff reviewed Onyx Arbor Hills Landfill's permit application (MI0045713) and found exceedances of CBOD5, ammonia, TSS, and total phosphorus in 2004–2005, primarily from compost operation discharges. Key data point: CBOD5, NH3, TSS, and Total P exceedances documented in 2004 and 2005 from compost operation discharges; facility uses ion exchange resin (substituting for activated carbon) and vegetated ditch for treatment.
