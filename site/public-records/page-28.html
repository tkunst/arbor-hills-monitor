2019-02-07 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · urgent

Violation Notice

DEQ issued a violation notice on February 7, 2019, finding that Arbor Hills Landfill failed to maintain adequate backup flaring capacity due to blower malfunctions that left the facility with only 3,000 scfm of flaring capacity instead of the required 9,000–10,000 scfm. The violation stems from a defective blower system installed in late 2018 and compounds the risk of odorous emissions during any outage at the adjacent gas-to-energy facility, which lacks backup generators.

Key data point: Flaring capacity reduced to 3,000 scfm (out of required 9,000–10,000 scfm) due to blower over-amping since December 2018; no backup power for flares or turbines.
