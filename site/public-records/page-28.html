2018-10-16 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · notable

Test Observation

EGLE Air Quality Division staff observed Day 1 of required compliance retesting of Fortistar's four landfill gas turbines at Arbor Hills on October 16, 2018. 2018-09-24 · Arbor Hills Landfill · opinion · routine

Violation Notice

This is a lawyer's response to a Notice of Violation (NOV) issued by Michigan DEQ in September 2018, alleging that Arbor Hills Landfill created a Level 3 'distinct and definite objectionable landfill garbage odor' in nearby residential areas in violation of state air quality rules. The operator argues that two years of RK & Associates odor monitoring (10,512 readings) showed minimal detectable odors, that the facility has substantially improved landfill gas collection efficiency since taking over the system in February 2017, and that the DEQ's use of a vague 'MDEQ-AQD Odor Scale' as enforcement basis violates due process.

Key data point: RK Associates monitoring (Nov 2016–Aug 2018): 89.3% of 10,512 readings showed zero odors; 10.2% <2 dilution factor; only three readings <7 dilutions, last on Feb 18, 2017. Two '>2' readings on Aug 23, 2018 (inspection date).

2018-08-30 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · notable

Violation Notice

The Michigan DEQ Air Quality Division issued a violation notice to FORTISTAR Methane Group / Arbor Hills Energy on August 30, 2018, documenting multiple exceedances of SO2 emission limits during performance testing in July 2018. The violations include failure to meet turbine and duct-burner SO2 caps, failure to complete required performance testing within the contractual window, operation of a gas collection control system out of compliance with federal standards, and an unpermitted PSD major modification resulting in a significant net SO2 emissions increase exceeding 40 tons per year.

Key data point: SO2 emissions during July 2018 testing: Turbine 1 at 3.90 lb/hr (permit limit 2.9 lb/hr); Turbine 2 at 5.47 lb/hr; Turbine 3 at 5.68 lb/hr; Ductburner 1 at 2.64 lb/hr (permit limit 0.3 lb/hr); Turbine 4 at 2.15 lb/MWhr (permit limit 0.9 lb/MWhr). Testing conducted with 25 landfill gas wells shut off.
