2021-12-03 · Arbor Hills Remediation Area · evidence · notable

nForm Document

This is a First Interim Short-Term Stormwater Characterization Study (STSWCS) Report filed by Arbor Hills Landfill Inc. in December 2021 to characterize PFAS (perfluoroalkyl and polyfluoroalkyl substances) sources in stormwater at the facility. The study evaluated two qualifying rain events in September and October 2021 and identified PFAS-containing aqueous film-forming foam (AFFF) discharged by local fire department during a 2016 recycling facility fire, as well as consumer waste, as potential PFAS sources. Samples from multiple on-site and off-site locations were analyzed, with nine of eleven roadway drainage ditch samples exceeding Michigan's Rule 57 Human Noncancer Value for PFOS (12 ppt/12 ng/L).

Key data point: Nine of eleven roadway drainage ditch samples exceeded Michigan Rule 57 HNV for PFOS (12 ng/L); PFOS readings ranged 27–56 ng/L in SW and NW roadway ditches on 9/22/2021 and 10/21/2021; AFFF source from 2016 fire confirmed in Pond 1, Pond 2, and Wetland #1.

Arbor Hills Landfill responds to an EGLE Violation Notice dated November 10, 2021, challenging two alleged violations: (1) that the gas control system is not designed to handle maximum expected gas flow, and (2) that Flare 391 failed to start during an October 19–20 plant outage. 2021-10-08 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · urgent

On-Site Inspection

On October 8, 2021, EGLE-AQD inspectors conducted an announced compliance inspection of the Arbor Hills Energy LLC facility (the gas-to-energy operation at the Arbor Hills Landfill). The inspection focused on ambient air testing for methane and hydrogen sulfide and discovered significant undocumented landfill gas emissions from multiple sources on the treatment and turbine building roofs, including a passive sewer vent measuring 129,000 ppm methane and H2S exceeding 50 ppm, and cooling vents on the turbine building roof emitting up to 1,500 ppm methane. The inspectors concluded that the amount and concentration of landfill gas detected was beyond expectations and concerning, and noted that condensate volumes processed through the treatment building have grown dramatically from under 50,000 gallons per month in 2018 to over 350,000–450,000 gallons per month by mid-2021.

Key data point: Methane measured at 129,000 ppm from condensate sump passive vent on treatment building roof; H2S exceeded 50 ppm detection limit (both unquantified); 1,500 ppm methane from Turbine 2 cooling vent; condensate volume increased from <50k gal/month (2018) to 350k–450k gal/month (2021). October 8, 2021.
