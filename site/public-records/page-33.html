2016-03-15 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · urgent

Violation Notice

MDEQ issued a Violation Notice on March 15, 2016 citing Arbor Hills Landfill for Rule 433(1) violations due to ongoing odor nuisance detected off-site. Despite a corrective action plan submitted in February 2016, odors persisted through early March with close to 150 community complaints received. Key data point: 150+ odor complaints from nearby residential areas; odors detected off-site on 8 separate inspection dates (Feb 16–Mar 8, 2016) in violation of Rule 433(1) (gases must not create nuisance at property boundary).

2016-02-16 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · notable

On-Site Inspection

On February 16, 2016, the US EPA Region V conducted an unannounced compliance inspection of Arbor Hills Landfill accompanied by Michigan AQD and OWMRP staff. The inspection focused on ongoing odor complaints from nearby residential areas, particularly in Cell 4 (newly constructed in 2014), and included geospatial air monitoring for methane and hydrogen sulfide in downwind and residential areas. AQD and EPA staff detected landfill gas odors at multiple locations on and off-site, though no Rule 901 violation had been formally verified at the time of inspection.

Key data point: Cell 4 (waste accepted May 2014, ~2 years old at inspection) identified as primary LFG odor source; 11 of 15 newly installed wells were online; ongoing odor complaints from east, northeast, and southeast residential subdivisions; no FLIR-detected gas leaks observed.
