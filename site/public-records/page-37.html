1997-11-20 · Arbor Hills Energy · procedural · routine

Air Permit to Install

This is a 1997 air permit supplement (913-90D) for Browning-Ferris Gas Services' landfill gas collection and thermal oxidation system at the Northville facility. It establishes emission limits for NOx, CO, SO2, HCl, and VOCs from flares, turbines, and duct burners, along with monitoring and operational requirements including continuous temperature monitoring of flares and sulfur content analysis of fuel.

Key data point: No measured data presented; this is a permitted ceiling document. Key limits include: flare NOx max 20.0 lb/hr, CO max 54.9 lb/hr, total system NOx max 165.6 tons per 12-month rolling period; flare minimum operating temperature 1600°F.

1996-12-23 · Arbor Hills Energy · procedural · routine

Air Permit to Install

This is a 1996 air permit supplement for Browning-Ferris Gas Services' landfill gas collection and thermal oxidation system at Northville, Michigan. The permit establishes emission limits for NOx, CO, SO2, HCl, and VOCs from flares, turbines, and duct burners, along with monitoring and operational requirements including continuous temperature monitoring and hydrogen sulfide fuel analysis.

Key data point: Thermal oxidizer minimum operating temperature 1600°F, minimum retention time 0.5 seconds; continuous temperature monitoring required.

1996-06-14 · Arbor Hills Energy · procedural · routine

Air Permit to Install

This is a 1996 air permit supplement for Browning-Ferris Gas Services' landfill gas collection and thermal oxidation system at the Northville, Michigan site. It establishes emission limits for NOx, CO, SO2, HCl, and VOC from flares, turbines, and duct burners, along with monitoring and operational requirements including continuous temperature monitoring and flame detection systems.

Key data point: Thermal oxidizer must maintain minimum 1600°F and 0.5 seconds retention time; continuous temperature monitoring required.
