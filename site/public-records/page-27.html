2019-02-07 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · urgent

Violation Notice

DEQ issued a violation notice on February 7, 2019, finding that Arbor Hills Landfill failed to maintain adequate backup flaring capacity due to blower malfunctions that left the facility with only 3,000 scfm of flaring capacity instead of the required 9,000–10,000 scfm.
