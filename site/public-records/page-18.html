2021-06-09 · Arbor Hills Remediation Area · procedural · routine

Compliance Communication

EGLE denied Arbor Hills Landfill's request to use compost leachate as a dust suppressant on landfill access roads. The denial was based on Part 115 Solid Waste Management rules, which prohibit disposal of liquid waste unless it meets specific exceptions for household waste, leachate, or gas condensate. The compost residuals in question do not qualify for these exceptions and therefore cannot be used for dust control.

Key data point: Compost leachate dust-suppressant request denied; does not meet leachate definition under Rule 299.4103(s) and violates Rule 430 prohibition on liquid waste disposal.
