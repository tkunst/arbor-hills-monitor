2017-01-18 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · notable

On-Site Inspection

On January 18, 2017, EGLE's Air Quality Division (AQD) conducted a surface inspection of Arbor Hills Landfill with RK Associates to evaluate odor and gas emissions across designated sections. The inspection identified multiple areas of methane gas migration, landfill gas (LFG) well maintenance issues, and strong odor plumes, including readings exceeding 20,000 ppm methane near an exposed gas line header west of the utility flare. The facility was found to be in compliance overall, though several specific conditions required follow-up corrective action.

Key data point: Methane concentration >20,000 ppm at exposed gas line header west of utility flare (Area H), and >500 ppm at multiple dead vegetation areas in Area G; LFG Well 288 hissing with bent top and trash showing through cover; visible emissions from LFG well with missing condensate collection hose, corrected on-site; odor level 2-3 detected near Cell 4 excavation and level 3 near leachate/flare area.

2017-01-05 · Arbor Hills Remediation Area · evidence · notable

AFFF used on fire

A fire at the Arbor Hills Materials Recovery Facility (MRF) in December 2016 triggered fire suppression activities. Stormwater runoff from the fire was contained in two ponds (Pond Napier and Pond 5 Mile) and tested for contaminants. Testing revealed elevated levels of PFOS (27,500 ng/L in Pond Napier; 2,500 ng/L in Pond 5 Mile), far exceeding the water quality standard of 11 ng/L, along with PFOA and other perfluorinated compounds. EGLE required treatment with multi-stage granular activated carbon and NPDES permit authorization before any discharge to state waters.

Key data point: PFOS concentration 27,500 ng/L in Pond Napier — 2,500× the water quality standard of 11 ng/L (December 5, 2016 lab report)
