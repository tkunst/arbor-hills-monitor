2019-01-08 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · urgent

On-Site Inspection

On January 8, 2019, EGLE conducted a compliance inspection of Fortistar Methane Group's Arbor Hills Energy LLC facility (the landfill gas energy plant at SRN N2688). The inspection found the facility in non-compliance with multiple air quality regulations, including documented SO₂ emission exceedances from stack testing in October 2018, unpermitted use of diesel fuel during turbine start-up, uncontrolled atmospheric vents from the treatment system, and violations of federal NSPS and NESHAP standards.

Key data point: October 16–18, 2018 stack testing: SO₂ exceeded permit limits by up to 159% for turbines (measured 4.4–7.5 lb/hr vs. 2.9 lb/hr limit) and 533% for duct burners (measured 1.6–1.9 lb/hr vs. 0.3 lb/hr limit).

2018-09-24 · Arbor Hills Landfill · opinion · routine

Violation Notice

This is a lawyer's response to a Notice of Violation (NOV) issued by Michigan DEQ in September 2018, alleging that Arbor Hills Landfill created a Level 3 'distinct and definite objectionable landfill garbage odor' in nearby residential areas in violation of state air quality rules. The operator argues that two years of RK & Associates odor monitoring (10,512 readings) showed minimal detectable odors, that the facility has substantially improved landfill gas collection efficiency since taking over the system in February 2017, and that the DEQ's use of a vague 'MDEQ-AQD Odor Scale' as enforcement basis violates due process.

Key data point: RK Associates monitoring (Nov 2016–Aug 2018): 89.3% of 10,512 readings showed zero odors; 10.2% <2 dilution factor; only three readings <7 dilutions, last on Feb 18, 2017. Two '>2' readings on Aug 23, 2018 (inspection date).
