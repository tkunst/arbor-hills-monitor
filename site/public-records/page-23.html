2019-10-22 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · notable

On-Site Inspection

EGLE conducted a three-day on-site inspection of Arbor Hills Landfill from October 22–24, 2019, in coordination with EPA Region V. The facility was found in non-compliance. Inspectors documented discussions of leachate management challenges, gas collection system upgrades, odor control measures, and subsurface gas migration issues near the northern perimeter.

Key data point: Methane readings as high as 3000 ppm and H2S as high as 0.3 ppm detected above the West haul road in small vents and erosion cracks; well 170R2 found very odorous with top partially dislodged; facility in non-compliance status.

2019-10-22 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · notable

Violation Notice

EGLE issued a violation notice on October 22, 2019, documenting ongoing failures in the landfill gas collection system (GCCS) at Arbor Hills, specifically high liquid levels in gas wells that impair gas collection and have worsened since 2016 despite a 2019 dewatering program. The violation cites federal NSPS and NESHAP standards and notes that 23 wells are pinched or otherwise impaired, with surface methane readings exceeding 500 ppm at numerous locations.

Key data point: Liquid levels in approximately 300 gas wells showed substantial increase over 3-month period (Q2–Q3 2019), ongoing since at least 2016; 23 named wells pinched/impaired; methane surface breakouts measured exceeding 500 ppm regulatory limit; violation previously cited 3/14/19 and by USEPA 9/29/16.

2019-10-21 · evidence · notable

Violation Notice

EGLE issued a violation notice to Advanced Disposal Services for impaired gas collection and control system performance at Arbor Hills Landfill, citing high liquid levels in gas wells that have worsened since 2016 despite a new dewatering program implemented in spring 2019. The violation involves failure to meet federal NSPS and NESHAP standards; approximately 300 wells showed substantial liquid-level increases over a 3-month period in Q2–Q3 2019, with 22 wells identified as pinched or otherwise impaired. The company was ordered to submit a corrective action plan by November 12, 2019.

Key data point: Approximately 300 gas wells showed substantial increase in liquid levels over 3-month period (Q2–Q3 2019); 22 named wells pinched or impaired; violation ongoing since at least 2016; methane levels measured exceeding 500 ppm regulatory limit at multiple surface locations.
