2025-10-06 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · urgent

SEM Inspection Report

EGLE conducted a September 25, 2025 Surface Emission Monitoring (SEM) inspection of Arbor Hills Landfill and identified 24 locations with methane concentrations exceeding 500 ppm above background. The inspection found widespread cover integrity issues, including erosion rills, bare ground, and surface penetrations, as well as substantial impairment of gas collection wells due to excess liquid levels and damaged infrastructure. The facility is experiencing ongoing fugitive methane and hydrogen sulfide emissions linked to perimeter air monitor exceedances and approximately 50–100 monthly odor complaints.

Key data point: 24 SEM hits at ≥500 ppm methane above background; 27 wells with <10" water column vacuum; 13 wells fully submerged; 44 additional wells 75–95% screen submerged; one probe (AHTGP18D) at 1458% LEL offsite; 27 wells with H₂S >1000 ppm; monthly methane action level exceedances (>40 ppm over 15 min) at perimeter monitors since May 2022.
