Site

The Conservancy Initiative submitted a letter to EGLE and stakeholders documenting a pattern of PFAS contamination at Arbor Hills Landfill from multiple sources: impacted groundwater wells (2019), stormwater ponds contaminated by aqueous firefighting foam (2016 fire), and a leachate spill into Johnson Creek (August 2022).
