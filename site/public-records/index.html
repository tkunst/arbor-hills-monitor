This EGLE Air Quality Division Records Review documents an exchange between EGLE and GFL (Arbor Hills Landfill, N2688) over a decline in landfill gas delivered to the Emerald RNG plant and flares even as GFL's LandGEM model predicts rising gas generation. EGLE's September 24, 2026 letter lays out data showing collected gas down ~6% and RNG-bound gas down ~15% from 2024-2026 while modeled generation rose ~2%, methane content of collected gas rising (not falling as GFL claimed), widespread wellfield impairment (liquid-impaired, air-infiltrated, vapor-locked, low-vacuum wells), and a June 16, 2026 inspection finding 75 surface locations at or above 500 ppm methane above background. After reviewing GFL's November 6, 2026 response, EGLE found GFL had not adequately explained the decline, had understated it by excluding 2023 data, and that waste receipts were running 19-33% below the tonnage GFL's gas model assumed.

Key data point: EGLE's June 16, 2026 facility inspection found 75 surface locations at or above 500 ppm methane above background at Arbor Hills Landfill.
