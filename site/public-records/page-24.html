An EGLE Air Quality Division compliance inspection of Arbor Hills Energy LLC (AHE, the gas-to-energy operator at SRN N2688) conducted November 14, 2019 found the facility in non-compliance with multiple federal and state air pollution regulations. Key data point: SO2 stack test results from October 16–18, 2018 showed exceedances of 100–159% over the 2.9 lb/hr limit for turbines (measured 4.4–7.5 lb/hr) and up to 533% over the 0.3 lb/hr limit for duct burners (measured 1.6–1.9 lb/hr).

2019-10-22 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · notable

Violation Notice

EGLE issued a violation notice on October 22, 2019, documenting ongoing failures in the landfill gas collection system (GCCS) at Arbor Hills, specifically high liquid levels in gas wells that impair gas collection and have worsened since 2016 despite a 2019 dewatering program. The violation cites federal NSPS and NESHAP standards and notes that 23 wells are pinched or otherwise impaired, with surface methane readings exceeding 500 ppm at numerous locations.

Key data point: Liquid levels in approximately 300 gas wells showed substantial increase over 3-month period (Q2–Q3 2019), ongoing since at least 2016; 23 named wells pinched/impaired; methane surface breakouts measured exceeding 500 ppm regulatory limit; violation previously cited 3/14/19 and by USEPA 9/29/16.
