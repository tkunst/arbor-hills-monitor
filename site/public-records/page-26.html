2019-03-14 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · urgent

Violation Notice

The Michigan DEQ Air Quality Division issued a comprehensive violation notice on March 14, 2019, documenting multiple violations of the Clean Air Act, Part 55, and various federal air pollution control rules at Arbor Hills Landfill (SRN N2688). The violations span asbestos disposal practices, municipal solid waste landfill gas collection and control systems, inadequate surface methane monitoring, landfill cover integrity failures, and gas collection well deficiencies including excessive liquid saturation. Additionally, the notice identifies ongoing odor problems, subsurface methane migration, and operational concerns with flares and treatment systems.

Key data point: 151 of 215 gas collection wells (70%) had more than 50% of their perforated screen submerged in leachate as of September 2018; 75 wells were more than 75% blocked and 35 fully saturated—documented as significantly degrading the effectiveness of the entire landfill gas collection system.

2019-03-13 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · urgent

Violation Notice

The Michigan DEQ Air Quality Division issued a violation notice on March 14, 2019 to Fortistar Methane Group and Advanced Disposal Services for multiple permit violations at Arbor Hills Landfill (SRN N2688). Violations include inadequate quarterly surface methane scans, failure to timely submit alternative compliance timeline requests for out-of-range NSPS well operating parameters, and widespread leachate saturation of gas collection wells that impairs system effectiveness. As of the notice date, previous violations from August 28, 2018 and February 2, 2019 remained unresolved.

Key data point: 151 of 215 gas collection wells had more than 50% of perforated screen submerged in liquid (75 wells >75% blocked, 35 fully saturated); as of September 2016 only 70 wells had this problem, indicating significant degradation. Multiple wells exceeded NSPS operating parameters (excess oxygen, temperature exceedances) with denied alternative compliance timeline requests.
