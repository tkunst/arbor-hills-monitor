2019-04-02 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · urgent

On-Site Inspection

EGLE conducted an unannounced compliance inspection on April 2, 2019, at Arbor Hills Landfill (N2688) and found evidence of an Elevated Temperature Landfill (ETLF) event with subsurface oxidation in approximately 35 acres, including an alarming carbon monoxide reading of 14,400 ppm from well AHW272R2 in January 2019 and a ground temperature of approximately 170°F near the subsidence area. The inspector documented multiple ETLF indicators including elevated well temperatures since 2017, increased hydrogen in landfill gas, rapid settlement, increased leachate volume, and pressurized leachate geysers, and recommended EPA involvement due to the severity and complexity of the situation.

Key data point: 14,400 ppm carbon monoxide from AHW272R2 (January 2019); ~170°F ground temperature 18 inches deep in subsidence area; ~35 acres with elevated well temperatures; compliance status: Non-Compliance

2019-03-14 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · urgent

Violation Notice

The Michigan DEQ Air Quality Division issued a comprehensive violation notice on March 14, 2019, documenting multiple violations of the Clean Air Act, Part 55, and various federal air pollution control rules at Arbor Hills Landfill (SRN N2688). The violations span asbestos disposal practices, municipal solid waste landfill gas collection and control systems, inadequate surface methane monitoring, landfill cover integrity failures, and gas collection well deficiencies including excessive liquid saturation. Key data point: 151 of 215 gas collection wells (70%) had more than 50% of their perforated screen submerged in leachate as of September 2018; 75 wells were more than 75% blocked and 35 fully saturated—documented as significantly degrading the effectiveness of the entire landfill gas collection system.
