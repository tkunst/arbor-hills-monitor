2019-06-18 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · urgent

On-Site Inspection

On June 18, 2019, EGLE conducted an unannounced compliance inspection of Arbor Hills Landfill and found non-compliance status. The inspector documented multiple air quality and odor violations, including elevated hydrogen sulfide levels, methane seeps exceeding 10,000 ppm over a 1-acre area, intense odors from frac tanks and active faces, and leachate seeps extending over 100 yards. The facility was also operating a candlestick flare due to excess gas beyond Fortistar's utilization capacity, and a pinched-off gas well vacuum line was discovered during the walk.

Key data point: Elevated methane levels of several hundred ppm covering at least 1 acre area, with one spot exceeding 10,000 ppm; elevated H2S throughout multiple areas; leachate seep extending >100 yards; pinched-off gas well vacuum line (possibly well 149R2) causing uncontrolled gas release; non-compliance status cited, June 18, 2019.
