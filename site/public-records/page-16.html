2022-03-07 · Arbor Hills Landfill · procedural · urgent

Consent Judgement

This is the 2020-0593-CE Consent Judgment between EGLE and Arbor Hills Landfill, filed March 7, 2022, settling allegations of violations of air, water, and solid waste rules from January 2018 through November 2019, including odor nuisance, gas collection system deficiencies, leachate management failures, and elevated landfill gas temperatures. The judgment imposes operational and monitoring requirements on the landfill including temperature controls on gas collection wells (capped at 55°C), leachate management, and perimeter air quality monitoring, with no admission of liability by the defendant.

Key data point: Wells at the Landfill had landfill gas temperatures that exceeded 55°C (131°F), triggering violations of federal NESHAP standards, federal NSPS, Rule 902, and Renewable Operating Permit MI-ROP-N2688-2011a.
