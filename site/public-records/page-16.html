Consent Decree

Automated summary of the linked document above. This summary and any key data point below are machine-generated and may contain errors. Consult the source document before relying on them.

This is a First Amendment to a December 2021 federal Consent Decree between the United States, Michigan EGLE, Arbor Hills Energy LLC, and Arbor Hills RNG LLC, modifying compliance requirements for landfill gas emissions at the Arbor Hills site. The amendment shifts the operator from an initially selected SO2-limiting Sulfur Treatment System pathway to a Renewable Natural Gas (RNG) facility pathway, and delays the final compliance deadline from March 31, 2023 to October 31, 2023 due to COVID-19 supply chain impacts. Between May 15 and October 30, 2023, an interim hydrogen sulfide treatment requirement (20 ppmv or less) is imposed during the transition period.

Key data point: H2S concentration standard of 20 ppmv or less required for Interim LFG Treatment Period between May 15, 2023 and October 30, 2023; RNG Option Final Compliance Deadline extended to October 31, 2023.
