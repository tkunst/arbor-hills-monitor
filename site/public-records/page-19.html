On-Site Inspection

On March 25, 2021, Michigan EGLE conducted a Surface Emission Monitoring (SEM) survey of Arbor Hills Landfill's north side and detected 8 locations with methane concentrations exceeding 500 ppm, the federal regulatory threshold under 40 CFR 60.753(d). The facility was found in overall compliance; however, corrective actions including cover maintenance and re-monitoring within 10 days were required at the exceedance locations. Key data point: 8 surface methane exceedances detected above 500 ppm threshold (ranging 639–7658 ppm), with highest reading of 7658 ppm at East side of Cell 4E, March 25, 2021; H2S elevated above 20 ppb in NW corner.

2020-10-31 · Arbor Hills Remediation Area · evidence · notable

Arbor Hill LF sampling report obtained by WRD.

This PFAS Site Characterization Report, prepared by Golder Associates for Arbor Hills Sanitary Landfill in October 2020, documents a comprehensive investigation of per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) detected in groundwater, surface water, sediment, and leachate at the site. Monitoring well MPB-393 contained PFOA, PFOS, and PFHxS at concentrations exceeding Michigan's drinking water criteria (DWC), and multiple potential contamination sources were identified, including legacy fire-fighting foam use at adjacent Great Lakes Recycling, leachate management system failures, and potential sanitary sewer line seepage. A PFAS treatment system was installed on-site by September 2020 to remove PFAS from leachate before discharge.

Key data point: Groundwater at well MPB-393 contained PFOA at 26 ng/L (exceeds 8 ng/L DWC), PFOS at 49 ng/L (exceeds 16 ng/L DWC), and PFHxS at 210 ng/L (exceeds 51 ng/L DWC) as of April 2, 2020; surface water samples from Detention Pond showed PFOS up to 400 ng/L (exceeds 12 ng/L Rule 57 limit); sediment from detention pond stilling basin exceeded PFOS GSIP criterion at 0.24 µg/kg.
