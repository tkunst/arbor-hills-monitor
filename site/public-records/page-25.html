On October 8, 2021, EGLE-AQD inspectors conducted an announced compliance inspection of the Arbor Hills Energy LLC facility (the gas-to-energy operation at the Arbor Hills Landfill). The inspection focused on ambient air testing for methane and hydrogen sulfide and discovered significant undocumented landfill gas emissions from multiple sources on the treatment and turbine building roofs, including a passive sewer vent measuring 129,000 ppm methane and H2S exceeding 50 ppm, and cooling vents on the turbine building roof emitting up to 1,500 ppm methane. October 8, 2021.
