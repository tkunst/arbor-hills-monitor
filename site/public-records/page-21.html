On-Site Inspection

On October 28, 2020, EGLE conducted a Surface Emission Monitoring (SEM) survey at Arbor Hills Landfill and found 33 locations with methane concentrations exceeding the 500 ppm regulatory threshold. The survey revealed multiple exceedances at active gas collection wells, erosion rills, and damaged infrastructure, indicating non-compliance with federal Clean Air Act standards. Corrective actions including cover maintenance, vacuum adjustments, and potential installation of new collection devices are required within regulatory timelines.

Key data point: 33 methane exceedances detected on 10/28/2020 including Well 265R2 at 114,995 ppm, Well 264R2 at 53,388 ppm, and Well 5-01 (damaged cap, visible gas/odor) at 32,873 ppm; downwind reading 14 ppm on Napier Road with strong sewage smell; COMPLIANCE STATUS: Non-Compliance.

2020-07-28 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · notable

Violation Notice

EGLE issued a violation notice to Arbor Hills Landfill on July 28, 2020, for impaired gas collection and control system performance due to high liquid levels in gas wells. The problem, first identified in 2016, has worsened despite a dewatering program implemented in spring 2019, with 185 vertical wells more than 50% blocked by liquid and 48 wells 100% submerged. A June 25, 2020 methane surface emission monitoring survey found 19 areas with concentrations exceeding 500 ppm, indicating continued system failure.

Key data point: As of January-April 2020: 185 of vertical gas wells had >50% screen blocked by liquid; 48 wells 100% submerged; 73 wells needed complete replacement; nearly half of 192 equipped pumps had operational problems; June 25, 2020 SEM survey found 19 surface methane exceedances >500 ppm.
