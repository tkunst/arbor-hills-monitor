2020-07-28 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · notable

Violation Notice

EGLE issued a violation notice to Arbor Hills Landfill on July 28, 2020, for impaired gas collection and control system performance due to high liquid levels in gas wells. The problem, first identified in 2016, has worsened despite a dewatering program implemented in spring 2019, with 185 vertical wells more than 50% blocked by liquid and 48 wells 100% submerged. A June 25, 2020 methane surface emission monitoring survey found 19 areas with concentrations exceeding 500 ppm, indicating continued system failure.

Key data point: As of January-April 2020: 185 of vertical gas wells had >50% screen blocked by liquid; 48 wells 100% submerged; 73 wells needed complete replacement; nearly half of 192 equipped pumps had operational problems; June 25, 2020 SEM survey found 19 surface methane exceedances >500 ppm.
