Landfill gas laboratory analyses (June 4, 2024 samples)

Laboratory results for two gas samples taken on June 4, 2024 for the gas-migration investigation at the Arbor Hills Landfill: one from gas well AHEW57R2 in the Arbor Hills East landfill and one from temporary gas probe AHTGP18D, east of Napier Road.

Each row is one analyte in one sample, as printed in Table 3 of SCS Engineers’ 2024 Gas Migration Investigation Report: 68 volatile organic compounds (EPA Method TO-15, in ppbv), six fixed gases (EPA Method 3C, in percent), 23 reduced sulfur compounds and totals (ASTM D-5504, in ppmv), and the field-meter readings taken when the samples were collected. Every non-detect carries the laboratory’s reporting limit. The laboratory’s scanned result tables were typed in by hand and the build checks them against Table 3. The README in the download explains each column and what the numbers can and cannot show.

SubjectArbor Hills Landfill, Salem Township, Washtenaw County, Michigan (SRN N2688)
