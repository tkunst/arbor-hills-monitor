Gas-migration borings, probes and passive vents (2020, 2024, 2026)

Where methane was looked for underground along Napier Road at the Arbor Hills Landfill, what the borings found, and how the gas probes and passive vents there were built, from three consultant reports filed with Michigan EGLE.

Five tables: the 24 borings, probes and vents with their dates, coordinates and depths; 267 methane readings by depth at borings X17–X22 (2020) and X23–X30 (2024); construction details of eight temporary gas probes installed in 2020 and two passive vents installed in September 2026; depth to liquid and screen condition at six probes and wells in June and August 2024; and the soil units described on the 2020 boring logs. All values are as printed. Each report prints coordinates its own way, and methane was measured differently in each program; the README in the download quotes each method.

SubjectArbor Hills Landfill, Salem Township, Washtenaw County, Michigan (SRN N2688)
What it coversBorings X17–X30, temporary gas probes TGP17D–TGP22, passive vents AEPV15R2 and AHEPV18D; methane by depth, soil units, construction, probe water levels
Time spanSeptember 2020, June and August 2024, September 2026
SourceEIL, LLC, Supplemental Methane Monitoring Probe Installation Report (November 5, 2020) and SCS Engineers, 2024 Gas Migration Investigation Report (October 9, 2024), released by EGLE under FOIA E701381-110226; SCS Engineers, Gas Migration Remedial Activities Status Update, Q3 2026 (October 30, 2026), released under FOIA E700318-100826. The bundle holds 39 selected pages; the full reports are in the public records
