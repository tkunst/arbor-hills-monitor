Landfill gas collected, daily (June 2023 to September 2026)

Daily landfill-gas flow from the Arbor Hills Landfill gas collection system to the renewable natural gas plant, the turbine plant and GFL’s landfill flares, read from the gas plants’ monthly reports to Michigan EGLE, with the 40 source PDFs and a reproducible parser.

Each row is one day: the flow to each destination in standard cubic feet per day, wellfield vacuum, methane percent and heat content, as reported, plus a computed total of the three flows. The table covers every day from June 1, 2023 to September 30, 2026 (1,218 days). A monthly summary is included. Flows are as reported, with no moisture adjustment; only the September 2026 report states a dry or wet basis. The heat column measures different things in different report layouts; the README in the download explains each.

SubjectArbor Hills Landfill, Salem Township, Washtenaw County, Michigan (SRN N2688)
What it coversDaily landfill-gas flow to the RNG plant (Emerald RNG LLC, EURNGPLANT), the turbine plant (Arbor Hills Energy LLC, FGPROJECT23) and GFL’s landfill flares; wellfield vacuum, methane percent and heat content
