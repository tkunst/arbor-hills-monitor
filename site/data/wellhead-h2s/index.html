Wellhead hydrogen-sulfide survey (2026)

The operator's own per-well wellhead hydrogen-sulfide readings from its 2026 annual survey at the Arbor Hills Landfill — all 506 wells — published with the source PDF and a reproducible parser.

This is the reading-level record behind the monitor's hydrogen sulfide page. In the surveyed gas, 261 of the 506 wells were at or above 76 ppm and 91 were at or above 500 ppm, with the strongest source at a leachate sump. These are in-gas source-strength readings measured inside the wells — a confined-space hazard, not resident exposure. Every value can be checked back to the operator's filed survey.
