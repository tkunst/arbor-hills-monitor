Perimeter air-monitoring dataset

Fence-line methane and hydrogen-sulfide readings from the six-station perimeter network (MS-1 through MS-6) around the Arbor Hills Landfill, May 2022 to September 2026. Published with a raw snapshot so the enriched table can be rebuilt and checked offline.

This is the ambient, at-the-boundary record behind the monitor's hydrogen sulfide page — the concentrations that actually reach the property line, in parts per billion, as distinct from the far stronger in-gas readings inside the wells. The dataset carries the raw values, so the sensor problems described below are visible in it.

Data-quality note (updated September 25, 2026): only two of the six fence-line stations, MS-3 and MS-4, are currently producing usable hydrogen-sulfide readings. MS-2, historically the station with the most exceedances, reported a flat 0.0 ppb from June 30, 2025 until two small readings (0.1 and 0.5 ppb) on September 24–25, 2026; whether its sensor has been repaired is not yet clear. MS-5 reported a flat 0.0 ppb from May 28, 2025 and has sent no data since September 16, 2026. MS-6 has reported a flat 3.0 ppb since June 11, 2026 (3.1 ppb since September 23). MS-1 was working normally but has sent no data since September 17, 2026. A working sensor produces small, fluctuating values, not an unbroken run of one number, so these flat stretches point to non-responsive or uncalibrated sensors rather than clean air, and a quiet record at those stations is not verified compliance. The working stations did record readings above the 30 ppb level on September 14, 2026. Status as of September 25, 2026, 10:00 a.m. ET. Source: GFL's own public perimeter feed (Barr Engineering).
