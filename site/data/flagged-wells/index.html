Flagged wells: elevated-temperature reading history

A smaller, curated subset of the wellfield dataset — the elevated-temperature reading history for the flagged wells at the Arbor Hills Landfill, against the subsurface-temperature-event thresholds.

This subset pulls out the wells with an elevated-temperature reading history from the full wellfield gas & temperature dataset, for anyone who wants the temperature story without the whole table. It is the shortlist behind the monitor's thermal map. Every reading traces back to the operator's EGLE filings.
