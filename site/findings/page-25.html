2019-04-25 · evidence · urgent

On-Site Inspection

EGLE conducted an unannounced compliance inspection of Arbor Hills Landfill on April 25, 2019, focusing on air quality permit compliance, turbine specifications, and ongoing investigation of elevated-temperature landfill-fill (ETLF) conditions. The inspection documented active subsurface oxidation indicators, including steam and gas venting at the leachate treatment pond (TS-01), elevated hydrogen sulfide readings, elevated waste-mass temperatures, and multiple infrastructure failures (ruptured leachate header, failed pump, cracked storm-drain pipes). The facility was rated in Non-Compliance status.

Key data point: TS-01 leachate pond measured at 135°F (25°F increase from 110°F two weeks prior); hydrogen sulfide at 10 ppm (matching OSHA exposure limit); well WW272R2 gas temperature at 157°F with only 23.1% methane; evidence of subsurface gas breakthrough and leachate seeps.
