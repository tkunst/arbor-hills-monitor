2021-10-08 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · urgent

On-Site Inspection

On October 8, 2021, EGLE-AQD inspectors conducted an announced compliance inspection of the Arbor Hills Energy LLC facility (the gas-to-energy operation at the Arbor Hills Landfill). The inspection focused on ambient air testing for methane and hydrogen sulfide and discovered significant undocumented landfill gas emissions from multiple sources on the treatment and turbine building roofs, including a passive sewer vent measuring 129,000 ppm methane and H2S exceeding 50 ppm, and cooling vents on the turbine building roof emitting up to 1,500 ppm methane. The inspectors concluded that the amount and concentration of landfill gas detected was beyond expectations and concerning, and noted that condensate volumes processed through the treatment building have grown dramatically from under 50,000 gallons per month in 2018 to over 350,000–450,000 gallons per month by mid-2021.

Key data point: Methane measured at 129,000 ppm from condensate sump passive vent on treatment building roof; H2S exceeded 50 ppm detection limit (both unquantified); 1,500 ppm methane from Turbine 2 cooling vent; condensate volume increased from <50k gal/month (2018) to 350k–450k gal/month (2021). October 8, 2021.

On-Site Inspection

An EGLE Air Quality Division inspector conducted a full compliance evaluation of Arbor Hills Energy LLC (Fortistar Methane Group) on September 24, 2021, finding the facility in compliance with most state and federal air regulations, but documenting ongoing sulfur dioxide (SO2) emission limit exceedances across multiple turbines and duct burners since at least 2015. The inspection also noted that GFL had recently assumed operation of approximately 22 landfill wells under Higher Operating Temperature (HOV) waiver status, and that a steam plant outage was impacting turbine operations at the time.

Key data point: All FGTURBINES and FGDUCTBURNERS and EUTURBINE4-S3 in continuous non-compliance with SO2 emission limits; facility-wide rolling 12-month SO2: 117.2 TPY measured versus ~29.5 TPY permitted (2021 data ending August); prior stack tests (Oct 2018) showed Turbine 1-3 results of 4.4, 6.3, 7.5 lb/hr SO2 against 2.9 lb/hr limit; Turbine 4 Sept 2020 test: 2.0 lb/MW-hr (limit 0.9).
