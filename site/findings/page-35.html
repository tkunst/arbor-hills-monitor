2005-12-09 · Arbor Hills Remediation Area · evidence · notable

Post-Inspection Report

A December 2005 DEQ Water Bureau post-inspection report documents a wastewater treatment facility (Onyx Arbor Hills LF) treating contaminated groundwater and compost runoff. The inspection on 10/25/05 found satisfactory conditions across most operational and monitoring areas. However, the report details 12 active effluent violations spanning from January 2004 to May 2005, involving exceedances of BOD, ammonia, total nitrogen, total solids, and phosphorus limits.

Key data point: 12 active effluent violations (BOD, ammonia, nitrogen, total solids, phosphorus) from January 2004 – May 2005; highest measured: 202 mg/l total solids (permit limit 30 mg/l), 111.1 lbs total solids (permit limit 25 lbs/day), ammonia 0.72 mg/l (permit limit 0.5 mg/l).

2005-11-28 · Arbor Hills Remediation Area · evidence · notable

Gen'l Inspection Transmittal

DEQ Water Bureau conducted a Compliance Sampling Inspection on October 25–26, 2005, of the remediation area east of Arbor Hills Landfill under NPDES Permit No. MI0045713. All tested parameters met effluent limitations at the time of sampling, but the site reported six exceedances in the prior 12 months (January–May 2005), primarily in Total Phosphorus and CBOD5, likely from compost pile runoff.

Key data point: Six NPDES permit exceedances reported in past 12 months: Total Phosphorus at 1.4 mg/L and 0.96 lbs/day (limits 1.0 mg/L and 0.8 lbs/day, January 2005); CBOD5 at 21 mg/L and 14.3 lbs/day (limits 10 mg/L and 8.3 lbs/day, May 18, 2005).

Pre-Inspection Report

Air Permit to Install
