2016-12-16 · Arbor Hills Remediation Area · evidence · notable

AFFF used on fire

Following a fire at the Arbor Hills Landfill recycling center in December 2016, stormwater pond sampling revealed PFOS and PFOA contamination from aqueous film-forming foam (AFFF) used in fire suppression. EGLE determined the contaminated water could not be discharged without multi-stage granular activated carbon treatment and an NPDES permit due to PFOS levels far exceeding the state water quality standard.

Key data point: PFOS concentrations of 27,500 ng/L in Pond Napier and 2,500 ng/L in Pond 5 Mile, both far exceeding the water quality standard of 11 ng/L, December 2016.
