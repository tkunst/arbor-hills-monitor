2020-02-27 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · notable

Violation Notice

On February 7–10, 2020, Arbor Hills Landfill failed to properly operate landfill gas flare controls, causing approximately 0.597 million cubic feet of collected landfill gas and an estimated 79.04 pounds of non-methane organic compounds (NMOC) to be released directly to the atmosphere instead of being incinerated. The malfunction violated federal Clean Air Act standards, state air rules, and the facility's permit conditions, and triggered 32 odor complaints during the release period.

Key data point: 79.04 pounds of NMOC emitted to atmosphere, Feb 7–10, 2020; 32 odor complaints received during the incident.
