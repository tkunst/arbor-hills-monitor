2019-12-19 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · routine

Site

This is a December 2019 flare performance test report for two enclosed landfill gas flares (Flare 391 and Flare 392) at Arbor Hills Landfill. Testing was conducted to demonstrate compliance with the Michigan Permit to Install (PTI 79-17) and EPA Subpart WWW standards, with measurements of NOx, CO, oxygen, and non-methane organic compounds collected during three test runs on December 19, 2019.

Key data point: Flare 391 (McGill) flare temperature averaged 1,520°F across three runs; Flare 392 (Zink) averaged 1,396°F at standard load and 1,479°F at maximum load; all emission parameters met permit limits including NOx 0.04 lb/mmBtu (limit 0.060) and CO 0.02–0.06 lb/mmBtu (limit 0.20).

2019-12-19 · Arbor Hills Landfill · evidence · notable

Test Observation

EGLE staff observed a stack test of two enclosed flares (391 and 392) at Arbor Hills Landfill on December 19, 2019, to verify compliance with NSPS Subpart WWW at increased operating capacities. The test proceeded with three runs after initial startup delays, and investigators documented operating temperatures, flow rates, and landfill gas odors throughout the facility, along with concerns about inconsistent semi-annual gas sampling results requiring resampling.

Key data point: Flare 391 measured at 1500F and 1490F during test runs; Flare 392 measured at 1352F and 1417F; both well above typical flare operating temperatures.
