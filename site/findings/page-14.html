Consent Decree

This is a First Amendment to a December 2021 federal Consent Decree between the United States, Michigan EGLE, Arbor Hills Energy LLC, and Arbor Hills RNG LLC, modifying compliance requirements for landfill gas emissions at the Arbor Hills site. The amendment shifts the operator from an initially selected SO2-limiting Sulfur Treatment System pathway to a Renewable Natural Gas (RNG) facility pathway, and delays the final compliance deadline from March 31, 2023 to October 31, 2023 due to COVID-19 supply chain impacts.
