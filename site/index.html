Arbor Hills Monitor

An independent, automated read of the public regulatory record for the Arbor Hills Landfill complex in Salem Township, Washtenaw County, Michigan.

GFL took ownership of Arbor Hills Landfill on October 30, 2020, per their regulatory submission to EGLE (Nov 4, 2020, SRN N2688, signed by David Seegert).

Several EGLE-regulated facilities sit on and around this one site, including the landfill, its groundwater Remediation Area, the Arbor Hills Energy plant, and the Emerald RNG plant. This project checks the state's public filing systems for every one of them each day, reads what's filed, and keeps a running, dated record of what it finds.

Public Records

  • Every document the monitor has read, from the earliest record to the most recent filing.

Public comment periods

  • Open public comment periods and deadlines: the EGLE applications and permit actions for these facilities that are open for public comment now, when each one closes, and a link to the official notice.

Data Visualizations

  • The wellfield thermal map: the operator's own EGLE-filed wellhead temperature readings, mapped well by well across the landfill.
  • The wellfield explorer: plot any well's EGLE-filed wellhead temperature, oxygen, and methane-to-carbon-dioxide ratio over time, well by well or by thermal cluster, against the subsurface-temperature-event thresholds.
  • The surface-methane exceedance map: every EGLE-documented surface-methane exceedance, stepped across six SEM reports and inspections and sized by concentration, with the SEM5000 walk tracks for the two EGLE targeted inspections.

The 2022 subsurface event

  • Photographs and video of the 2022 subsurface event on the landfill: a displaced gas well in subsided ground, vapor and pooled liquid, surface cracks, and infrared images of gas plumes. Public-information files, with their original timestamps and GPS.

The June 16, 2026 surface-methane inspection

  • EGLE's June 2026 SEM inspection: the Air Quality Division recorded 75 areas of surface methane above the 500-ppm threshold, up to 148,682 ppm at one well. Photographs from the inspection log, with EGLE's results letter and the full 94-photo log as public records.

Data releases

  • Wellfield gas and temperature dataset, 2021 to 2026: every reading, every well, with source PDFs and build scripts.
  • Perimeter air-monitoring dataset, May 2022 to August 2026: fence-line methane and hydrogen sulfide readings.
  • Flagged wells: elevated-temperature reading history, a smaller, curated subset of the wellfield dataset above.
