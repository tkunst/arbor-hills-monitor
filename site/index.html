Arbor Hills Monitor

An independent, automated read of the public regulatory record for the Arbor Hills Landfill complex in Salem Township, Washtenaw County, Michigan.

GFL took ownership of Arbor Hills Landfill on October 30, 2020, per their regulatory submission to EGLE (Nov 4, 2020, SRN N2688, signed by David Seegert).

Several EGLE-regulated facilities sit on and around this one site, including the landfill, its groundwater Remediation Area, the Arbor Hills Energy plant, and the Emerald RNG plant. This project checks the state's public filing systems for every one of them each day, reads what's filed, and keeps a running, dated record of what it finds.

The June 16, 2026 surface-methane inspection

  • EGLE's June 2026 SEM inspection: the Air Quality Division recorded 75 areas of surface methane above the 500-ppm threshold, up to 148,682 ppm at one well. Photographs from the inspection log, with EGLE's results letter and the full 94-photo log as public records.

The hydrogen sulfide record

  • The hydrogen sulfide record at Arbor Hills: the operator's own wellhead survey found hydrogen sulfide up to 30,000 ppm in the gas as recently as April 2026, with more than half of the 506 wells above the level dangerous if breathed. At the fence line it has reached 327 ppb, about 11 times the action level, between 2022 and 2025, while the school monitor next door has stayed below 1 ppb for 68 straight months.

Data-quality note (September 2026): two of the six fence-line stations — MS-2, historically the station with the most hydrogen-sulfide exceedances, and MS-5 — have reported a flat 0.0 ppb of hydrogen sulfide since mid-2025, while their methane sensors and the other four stations keep varying normally. A working sensor produces small non-zero readings, not an unbroken run of exact zeros, so this points to a non-responsive or uncalibrated H2S sensor rather than clean air — the recent absence of hydrogen-sulfide exceedances at those two stations is not verified compliance. Source: GFL's own public perimeter feed (Barr Engineering).
