Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Arbor Hills Monitor is committed to transparency about what information it collects and how that information is handled. This policy explains our data practices.

1. What we collect

Email addresses (alert subscribers). The Site operates an email alert system that notifies subscribers when new regulatory filings, exceedances, or significant developments are recorded in the Arbor Hills Landfill monitoring data. If you have asked to receive alerts, we hold your email address for that purpose.

How the list is maintained: recipients are added manually by the site operator on request. There is no automated self-service sign-up form. Your address is stored in a configuration file used by the alert-sending system.

Contact-form or email inquiries. If you contact us by email or through any contact mechanism, we receive and retain the email address and content of your message for the purpose of responding.

Usage data (Cloudflare). This site is served through Cloudflare, which provides aggregate visitor analytics -- for example, counts of unique visitors, page views, and referring sources. These analytics are reported to the operator in aggregate and are used only to understand overall traffic; we do not use them to identify individual visitors, and we do not run Google Analytics or similar advertising/tracking tools. As the network that delivers the site, Cloudflare also processes visitors' IP addresses and connection data in transit to route and protect the site, per Cloudflare's own privacy policy.

2. How we use your information

  • Email addresses in the alert list are used solely to send regulatory-monitoring alerts and occasional digests related to the Arbor Hills Landfill. We do not use them for any other purpose.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your email address or contact information with third parties, including advocacy organizations, government agencies, or the landfill operator.
  • Contact inquiries are used only to respond to you.

3. Who sees your information

The site operator ([PUBLISHER ENTITY -- TBD] / Trisha Kunst) has access to the alert-recipient list. Alert emails are sent via an SMTP email service; the credentials for that service are held by the operator.

4. Unsubscribing

To be removed from the alert list at any time, contact us at corrections@arborhillsmonitor.org. We will remove your address promptly. You may also reply "unsubscribe" to any alert email, or use the unsubscribe mechanism included in each message per CAN-SPAM requirements.

5. Retention

We retain alert-subscriber addresses as long as you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, your address is removed from the active list. Contact-inquiry emails are retained for unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the alert-recipient list from unauthorized access. The list is stored in a configuration file that is not published publicly.

7. Children

This Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Michigan residents

Michigan's data privacy law may provide additional rights regarding your personal information. Contact us to exercise any applicable rights.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: corrections@arborhillsmonitor.org
