Arbor Hills: two persistent thermal clusters

The operator's own EGLE-filed wellhead readings show the elevated-temperature wells (≥131°F) falling into two persistent clusters: an eastern hot core and a western warm field. Their nearest wells now sit about 460 ft apart, a gap the growing western field narrows after 2023 while the eastern core stays the larger and hotter.
